ELN is a digital tool that stands for *electronic lab notebook*. As digitalisation in scientific research is rapidly developing, nowadays most of the ELNs are not just merely tools for jotting down notes for what was done in the lab, but also are integrated with tools for analyses. 

The significance in the application of ELNs is tightly related to the [FAIR Principles](https://www.go-fair.org/fair-principles/) for scientific data management: **F**indability, **A**ccessibility, **I**nteroperability and **R**euse of digital assets. In short, we aspire to strengthen the network of data management and sharing among all fellow scientists.

## What is Kladde?
Kladde is a project supported in 2025 by the [Fellowships for Innovations in Digital Higher Education in Thuringia](https://www.stifterverband.org/digital-lehrfellows-thueringen), a programme coordinated by the Stifterverband für die Deutsche Wissenschaft e.V. The project aims to integrate the <gloss note="Open-source means that the source code of a programme is published, e.g. on GitHub, and is open for viewing and revision.">open-source</gloss> electronic lab notebook (ELN) SciPeaks, powered by [cheminfo](https://cheminfo.github.io/), into the practical curriculum for Bachelor’s students in Chemistry. Our vision is to establish a platform that offers both students and researchers a seamless workflow, from planning a synthesis through sample analysis to research data management.

SciPeaks is a browser-based integrated platform of ELN and multiple spectra simulation and analytical tools, from NMR (powered by [NMRium](https://app.nmrium.com/)), IR to MS. It also includes a big variety of tools for cheminformatics and biochemical analysis. 

There are currently a lot of choices of <gloss note="open-source means the source code of the programmes are published e.g. on GitHub and are open for viewing and revisions.">open-sourced</gloss> ELNs available among academic institutes, with which we are in close connection, such as [Chemotion](https://chemotion.net/) and [eLabFTW](https://www.elabftw.net/). The ultimate goal of these web applications is to introduce the use of digital application in scientific research and to promote a good practice in research data management that fits the FAIR principles.
