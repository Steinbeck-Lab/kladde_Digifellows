Luc Patiny’s EcoScale established a semi-quantitative tool for selecting organic preparations based on ecological and economical parameters. He is a researcher specializing in cheminformatics and the creation of open-access chemical infrastructure. Patiny co-developed OCHEM, a web-based platform for chemical data storage and model publishing, and ChemCalc, a modular system for chemical calculations. His work includes the development of nmrdb.org for the online processing of NMR spectra and algorithms for the accurate simulation of large spin systems. He also created PDB-Explorer for visualizing the Protein Data Bank in shape space. Patiny focuses on developing web platforms that make chemical knowledge machine-actionable and accessible for drug discovery and molecular modeling.
